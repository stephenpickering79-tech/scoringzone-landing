FAQ

The questions coaches
actually ask.

Will this replace me as their coach?
No. There is no swing instruction in Scoring Zone, no video analysis and no lessons. It scores the drills you set. It’s a practice log with a scoreboard, not a teacher — and it sends your student back to you with three weeks of data to talk about instead of a shrug.
Do I have to sell anything?
No. You give a student your code the same way you already recommend a glove or a putting mat. There’s no pitch, no quota, no minimum and no target. Most partner coaches mention it once, at the end of a lesson, and never bring it up again.
When do commissions actually start?
Scoring Zone is free while we finish the paid tier, so no commission has been paid yet — we’d rather say that plainly than let you find out later. Commissions begin the day paid subscriptions launch. Everyone who joins before then is locked at 50% permanently, rather than the standard rate. That’s the entire reason to join now rather than later.
How much can I realistically earn?
Scoring Zone is $9.99 a month. Your students pay $8.99 with your code and you keep 50% of that — $4.50 per student, every month they stay subscribed. Twenty-five students is roughly $112 a month, recurring, for work you’ve already done. It won’t replace your lesson income, and we’re not going to pretend it will — the reason to do this is what it does for your students.
How do I bring it up without sounding like a salesman?
The coaches who do this well don’t sell the app — they set homework. “I want you doing the gate drill three times before I see you again; put your scores in here so I can look at them.” The code is a detail at the end of a coaching instruction, not a product recommendation.
What if a student cancels?
The commission stops with the subscription. There’s no clawback and nothing is ever taken back from you — you keep everything already earned.
Is there a cost, a contract or a minimum?
None of the three. No joining fee, no card required, no minimum referrals, no exclusivity and no notice period. You can walk away whenever you like and still keep the commission on students who are already subscribed.
How and when do I get paid?
Monthly, by bank transfer, on the 1st — 30 days after the subscription is billed. You add your payout details in your dashboard whenever you like. Nothing financial is asked for on this page, and there’s no identity check to join.
Can I try the app myself first?
Please do — we’d rather you didn’t recommend it otherwise. It’s free to download at scoringzone.app, and joining the programme costs nothing either way.
