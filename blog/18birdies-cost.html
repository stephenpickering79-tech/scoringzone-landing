How Much Does 18Birdies Cost? Real 2026 Pricing for Every Tier

Free Forever, $99.99/Year, $19.99/Month or $7.99/Week — and Which One Is Worth It

August 16, 2026 · 7 min read · Stephen Pickering

Key takeaway: 18Birdies is free to download with no expiry. Premium is $99.99/year, $19.99/month or $7.99/week — and paying weekly for a full year costs $415.48 for the same app. The annual plan saves 58% over monthly. Premium buys better decisions, not better execution, so if your strokes are going inside 100 yards the free tier plus real short game practice covers more ground for nothing.

Straight answer: 18Birdies costs nothing to download, and 18Birdies Premium costs $99.99 a year, $19.99 a month, or $7.99 a week. The free tier never expires and never asks for a card.

Most articles about 18Birdies cost dance around this with a vague ”$60 to $90 depending on the plan”. That range is out of date. These are the prices listed on 18Birdies’ own pricing page as of August 2026, and the gap between the three plans is far bigger than most golfers realise — pay weekly for a year and you’ll spend more than four times the annual price for exactly the same app.

Here’s what each tier gets you, what the free version genuinely covers, and how to work out whether Premium earns its money for the way you play.

18Birdies Pricing in 2026: Every Plan

There are four ways to use 18Birdies. One of them is free forever.

The annual plan is the only sensible way to buy Premium. 18Birdies advertises it as saving “over 55% vs. monthly”, and the maths backs that up — $99.99 against $239.88 is a 58% saving.

The weekly plan is the one to be careful with. $7.99 looks harmless next to $99.99, which is exactly why it’s offered. Run it for twelve months and you’ve paid $415.48 for a golf app. It only makes sense if you genuinely intend to use it for a fortnight — a golf trip, a member-guest, a one-off tournament — and then cancel.

The 7-day free trial

Both the yearly and monthly plans come with a 7-day free trial. The weekly plan doesn’t.

The usual app-store rules apply: the trial rolls into a paid subscription automatically unless you cancel first. If you’re trialling it, set a reminder for day six rather than trusting yourself to remember on day seven. And trial it during a week you’re actually playing — a Premium trial burned on a rainy week tells you nothing.

Is 18Birdies Free? What You Get Without Paying

Yes, genuinely free — and this is the part worth understanding before you reach for a card.

18Birdies is free to download on iOS and Android. There’s no trial timer on the free tier, no card required at signup, and no feature that quietly expires after a fortnight. Free gets you:

  • GPS yardages to the front, centre and back of every green
  • A full digital scorecard with hole-by-hole entry
  • Basic round stats — fairways, greens, putts, score
  • Shot tracking
  • The social layer: group rounds, side games, leaderboards
  • Access to a course database covering tens of thousands of courses worldwide

The trade-off is ads. The free tier is ad-supported, and on an older phone they can be intrusive.

For a lot of golfers that’s the end of the conversation. If what you want is a yardage and a scorecard, 18Birdies free does that permanently, and millions of golfers use it for years without ever upgrading.

What 18Birdies Premium Actually Unlocks

Strip the marketing out and Premium adds on-course intelligence. Not swing help — information.

The headline features behind the paywall:

  • Wind, slope and conditions — factors slope, wind, rain, temperature and altitude into your playing yardage
  • Club recommendations — suggested club based on your own distances and the shot in front of you
  • 3D green maps — contours and putt break
  • Strokes gained — scoring metrics against a benchmark
  • Advanced stats — deeper charts with custom filters
  • AI swing analyser — unlimited swing uploads with drill suggestions
  • Tee shot planner, hole insights and shot dispersion
  • No ads

That’s a real feature list. It’s also, almost entirely, a list of things that help you *choose* better rather than *execute* better.

That distinction matters more than the price. If your 7-iron goes anywhere between 140 and 165 yards, being told to hit 7-iron doesn’t solve anything. If you’re leaving three shots a round around the green, a 3D map of the green’s contours doesn’t help you stop chunking the chip that got you there.

One thing to check before you rely on green reading

Slope and green-reading data is restricted in a lot of competition formats. If you play club competitions, check your committee’s local rule before you build a routine around a feature you can’t legally use when it counts.

Is 18Birdies Premium Worth $99.99 a Year?

Work it out per round rather than per year. That’s the number that actually tells you something.

At $2 a round it’s cheaper than a bag of range balls and easy to justify. At $10 a round you’re paying more for the app than most golfers spend on practice all month.

Premium is worth it if: you play 20-plus rounds a year, you have the app out on most holes anyway, the ads genuinely annoy you, and you’re a good enough ball-striker that better club selection changes your score.

Premium isn’t worth it if: you play under 10 rounds a year, you mostly want yardages and a scorecard, or — the big one — your scores are being lost inside 100 yards. No amount of on-course data fixes a short game. It just tells you about it in higher resolution.

The Cheaper Setup Most Golfers Should Try First

Before you spend $99.99, try this for a season and see whether you miss anything.

Run 18Birdies free for your yardages, your scorecard and your stats. It does that job well and it costs nothing. Then put the money you’d have spent on Premium into the part of the game where your strokes actually live — around 60% of every round happens inside 100 yards, and for a mid-handicapper that’s where four or five shots a round are quietly disappearing.

That’s the gap Scoring Zone was built to fill: scored short game drills with benchmarks, so you can see whether your chipping and putting are genuinely improving between rounds rather than guessing. It’s free for 7 days, which makes the combined setup cost nothing.

Two free apps covering on-course data and off-course improvement will move your handicap further than one paid app covering half of it. If after a season you find yourself genuinely wanting club recommendations and 3D greens, buy the annual plan then — you’ll know exactly why you’re paying.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does 18Birdies cost?

18Birdies costs nothing to download and the free tier has no time limit. 18Birdies Premium is priced at $99.99 per year, $19.99 per month, or $7.99 per week. The yearly and monthly plans both include a 7-day free trial; the weekly plan does not. The annual plan is by far the cheapest way to buy Premium — paying monthly for a full year costs $239.88, and paying weekly for a full year costs $415.48.

Is 18Birdies free?

Yes. 18Birdies is free to download on iOS and Android, and the free tier does not expire. You get GPS yardages to the front, centre and back of every green, a full digital scorecard, basic round stats, shot tracking, and the social and leaderboard features without paying anything. The free tier is ad-supported. You only pay if you want Premium features such as club recommendations, 3D green maps, strokes gained, and the AI swing analyser.

Does 18Birdies cost money to use every round?

No. There is no per-round charge and no limit on how many rounds you can score on the free tier. You are not asked for a card to sign up and the free features do not stop working after a set number of rounds. The only recurring cost is the optional Premium subscription.

Is 18Birdies Premium worth $99.99 a year?

It depends entirely on how much golf you play. At $99.99 a year Premium works out at roughly $2 per round if you play 50 rounds, and about $10 per round if you play 10. For a golfer playing 20-plus rounds a year who uses the app on every hole, the club recommendations, 3D green maps and ad-free experience are reasonable value. For a casual golfer, or for anyone whose scores are being lost around the green rather than in club selection, the free tier plus dedicated short game practice covers more ground for nothing.

Does 18Birdies Premium have a free trial?

Yes. The yearly and monthly Premium plans both include a 7-day free trial. The weekly plan has no trial. As with any app-store subscription, the trial converts to a paid subscription automatically unless you cancel before it ends, so set a reminder for day six.
