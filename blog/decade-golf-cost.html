DECADE Golf Cost: Real 2026 Prices for Every Tier

Breaking 90, Foundations and Elite Priced Up — Plus the Auto-Renew Clause to Watch

August 16, 2026 · 7 min read · Stephen Pickering

Key takeaway: DECADE Golf costs $89 (Breaking 90) to $325 (Elite annual), sold as six-month or annual memberships with no free tier or trial. Elite bought six-monthly auto-renews at $19.95/month, making a first year $369.70 against $325 for the annual plan. DECADE fixes target selection, not execution — worth it under 15 handicap, not before.

DECADE Golf costs between $89 and $325 depending on which tier you buy and whether you pay for six months or twelve. There’s no free tier, no free trial, and no cheap monthly plan — DECADE sells memberships, not a $10-a-month app subscription.

That surprises a lot of golfers who find DECADE through a YouTube clip of Scott Fawcett explaining why you should aim 20 feet left of a flag. The system is well regarded, and it’s used by serious amateurs and tour players. It’s also priced like a coaching product rather than a golf app, and there’s one auto-renew clause worth knowing about before you buy.

Here’s the full DECADE Golf cost breakdown as listed in August 2026, the six-month versus annual maths, and an honest read on who it’s actually worth it for.

DECADE Golf Pricing in 2026: Every Tier

DECADE sells three products across two billing terms.

Those are the prices listed on decade.golf. DECADE runs promotional pricing more or less permanently, with the discount applied at checkout, so what you actually pay may be lower. Check the cart total before you decide — the sticker price on the tier card is not always the final number.

There’s no free tier and no trial. You’re paying up front either way.

What each tier includes

DECADE Breaking 90 — $89 / six months. A cut-down, focused version: a dedicated Breaking 90 app, a course built specifically around getting under 90 consistently, quick stats entry, and one custom yardage book per month from DECADE’s 40,000-course database. This is the entry point, not the full system.

DECADE Foundations — $125 / six months or $199 / year. The main DECADE app, a six-month tailored course teaching the system properly, quick stats entry, one custom yardage book per month, and discounted rates on live seminars and camps.

DECADE Elite — $250 / six months or $325 / year. Everything in Foundations plus the full course management system, GPS shot entry, four yardage books a month, DECADELink hole-by-hole analysis, “Coaching Moments”, peer group comparison, and strokes gained against college and Top 50 amateur benchmarks. DECADE positions the deepest analysis at players averaging under 80.

The Auto-Renew Clause Worth Knowing About

This is the part that isn’t obvious from the pricing table, and it’s the reason people search for DECADE Golf cost twice.

Breaking 90 and Foundations six-month memberships auto-renew after six months. The annual plans auto-renew after twelve. Standard enough.

DECADE Elite bought on the six-month plan is different — it auto-renews at $19.95 per month.

Is DECADE Golf Worth the Money?

DECADE does one thing, and it does it genuinely well: it teaches you where to aim. It’s built on Scott Fawcett’s strokes gained work, and the core insight — that most amateurs lose shots by picking targets that don’t survive their own dispersion — is correct and under-appreciated.

It’s worth it if: you’re a sub-15 handicapper, you strike the ball reasonably well, and you know you make poor decisions on the course. Aiming at tucked pins. Taking on carries you convert three times in ten. Hitting driver on holes where it can’t help you. If that’s your round, DECADE will pay for itself.

It’s not worth it if: you’re above 15 and your problem is execution rather than target selection. Course management can only save shots you were about to throw away by choosing badly. It cannot save a chunked chip, a three-putt, or a wedge that comes up 15 yards short.

That distinction is where the money decision lives. For a 20-handicapper, the strokes are not hiding in strategy. Around 60% of every round happens inside 100 yards, and a mid-handicapper typically leaks four to five shots a round in that zone against a scratch player. Perfect target selection doesn’t touch a single one of them.

DECADE knows this, incidentally — the Elite tier openly reserves its deepest analysis for players averaging under 80. That’s a fair reflection of who the system is built for.

The honest sequencing

If you’re outside DECADE’s target golfer, the order matters more than the product. Fix the execution leak first, then buy the strategy system when better decisions actually have something to work with.

That means measuring your short game rather than guessing at it — up-and-down percentage, putts per round, three-putt count, proximity from 30 yards. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does DECADE Golf cost?

DECADE Golf is sold in six-month and annual memberships rather than a low monthly subscription. As listed on decade.golf in August 2026, the six-month tiers are DECADE Breaking 90 at $89, DECADE Foundations at $125, and DECADE Elite at $250. The annual tiers are Foundations at $199 and Elite at $325. There is no free tier and no free trial. DECADE also runs frequent promotional discounts applied at checkout, so the price you pay can be lower than the listed figure.

Is DECADE Golf worth it?

DECADE is worth it for a specific golfer: a sub-15 handicapper who strikes the ball well but consistently picks the wrong targets. It is a course management and strategy system built on Scott Fawcett’s strokes gained work, and it teaches you where to aim. It does not teach you how to chip, putt or control wedge distance. If you are losing four or five shots a round inside 100 yards, DECADE will not recover them — it is solving a decision problem, not an execution problem.

Does DECADE Golf have a free trial?

No. DECADE does not offer a free trial or a free tier. Every membership is paid up front for either six months or twelve months. DECADE does run regular promotional pricing with the discount applied at checkout, which is the closest thing to a cheaper way in.

Does DECADE Golf auto-renew?

Yes, and the terms differ by tier. Breaking 90 and Foundations six-month memberships auto-renew after six months, and the annual memberships auto-renew after twelve. The one to watch is DECADE Elite bought on the six-month plan: it auto-renews at $19.95 per month after the initial purchase, so a full first year on that route costs about $369.70 versus $325 for the annual Elite plan.

What is the cheapest DECADE Golf membership?

DECADE Breaking 90 at $89 for six months is the cheapest entry point. It is a cut-down version — a focused app and a course aimed specifically at breaking 90, with quick stats entry and one custom yardage book per month. It does not include the full DECADE course management system, the GPS entry, or the DECADELink analysis that comes with Elite.
